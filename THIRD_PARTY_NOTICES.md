# LogoLight

Portions of the CoreBrightness bridge and fade-control technique in `Sources/LidGlowUI/LogoHardware.swift` and `Sources/LidGlowCore/HardwareControl.swift` are adapted from LogoLight, https://github.com/Ark0N/LogoLight, revision `7018b92503f936cfc5956bef4a74cb730078a9d5`. Recovery-file validation, ownership, scheduling, and tests are LidGlow implementation work.
